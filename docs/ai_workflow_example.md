The crawler collects 15 job postings from Job Bank Canada automatically:
- worker-html finds job URLs

Objective: Perform a structured, factual analysis of hiring trends based strictly on the provided data.

### Key Highlights

- **Zimmer Air Services** and **IMP Aerospace and Defence** are the most active aviation employers with 2 postings each
- **St. Thomas, ON** is the only city with multiple aviation-specific postings (2), both from Zimmer Air Services
- **Quebec dominates volume** with 8 of 15 postings but these are composite/plastics manufacturing roles — not aviation maintenance
- **Salary data is incomplete** — 4 of 15 postings show "Not specified"; only 1 posting offers an annual salary
